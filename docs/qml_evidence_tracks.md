# Catalyst-Q QML Evidence Tracks

This repository is the public evidence factory for Catalyst-Q QML Proof Sprints.

## Quantum Oracle Sketching

Tests compact QML-style sketches for named classical datasets. Every result must include committed
inputs, baselines, validators, and a Quantum Execution Record or attested evidence artifact.

Primary artifacts: experiment protocol, baseline comparison, Catalyst-Q benchmark record, Quantum Execution Record, claim-boundary memo.

## Feature Encoding Workbench

Compares basis, angle, dense-angle, amplitude, IQP, and re-uploading encodings under explicit
resource and accuracy constraints.

Primary artifacts: encoding decision record, sweep report, Catalyst-Q benchmark record, Quantum Execution Record, claim-boundary memo.

## Trainability And Reservoir Track

Benchmarks candidate QML circuit families, residual trainability methods, and reservoir or
extreme-learning setups.

Primary artifacts: gradient map, reservoir benchmark report, Catalyst-Q benchmark record, Quantum Execution Record, claim-boundary memo.

## Claim boundary

Publish measured results for named workloads only. Do not publish broad SOTA, universal quantum
simulation, or category-leadership claims without External baselines and raw records.
